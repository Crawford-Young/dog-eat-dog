# CLAUDE.md — Dog Eat Dog

**Inherits:** `~/code/CLAUDE.md` + `~/code/games/CLAUDE.md`. Repo-specific rules only below.

## Repo Facts

- Godot binary comes from the `GODOT` env var; default in the Justfile points at the winget 4.7.1 console exe.
- After adding ANY `class_name` script: `just cache` before headless GUT (`--import` does not rebuild the class cache).
- After editing ANY `.tscn`/asset: `just import` before headless runs.
- Web export preset is committed (`export_presets.cfg`) — Web preset only, no secrets. `thread_support=false` is load-bearing (nothreads template = no COOP/COEP headers needed). `exclude_filter` keeps dev tooling (GUT addon, tests/, node_modules, package.json, .gutconfig.json, scenes/dev/, scripts/dev/) out of the pck — GUT scenes otherwise break the export with `Cannot set object script`; the lab shipped in wave-1 packs until the dev dirs were added (wave 2).
- `build/` carries a `.gdignore` (recreated by the `export-web` recipe — build/ is gitignored) so Godot never re-imports exported web icons as project resources.

## Game Rules That Bind Code

- **Energy is NEVER shown as a number** — the dog's sprite/state text IS the energy display.
- **No firing, no death** anywhere in systems or copy. Floor = demotion + sick day.
- Every minigame emits `finished(result)` with `{score: float 0..1, skipped: bool, failed: bool}` (`failed` defaults false); choice scenes add `"choice": String`.
- **Bedtime is derived, never chosen** — it is the evening `DayClock`'s bed minute at freetime end (`_on_finished`'s freetime branch), banded early / mid / late by `bed_early_line_minutes` (22:00) and `bed_mid_line_minutes` (23:30); the evening starts at `evening_start_minutes` (21:30). The sleep slot is a non-interactive narration beat, not a choice screen.
- Conversational copy types itself out — `Typewriter` (pure pacing) driven by a `TypewriterLabel` child node. Never set a bubble/prompt/summary `text` directly.
- All balance tunables live in `BalanceConfig` (`scripts/core/balance_config.gd` holds the defaults; `resources/balance_default.tres` carries overrides only and is currently zero-override) — feel iteration edits tunables, never minigame code.
- **All randomness comes from `RngSource`** (`scripts/core/rng_source.gd`) — `game.gd` owns one and hands every slot (cooking included) `params["rng"] = rng.next_rng()`; minigames read it via `RngSource.from_params`. No `randomize()` or bare `RandomNumberGenerator.new()` outside it, so a seed reproduces a run.
- Difficulty flows ONLY through `Difficulty.*_params(energy, cfg)` — no minigame reads GameState directly.

### Wave-2 invariants (economy + ladder)

- **Money is never negative** — all spending goes through `Wallet.spend`/`Wallet.drain`, both clamp at 0. Living costs drain at `close_day` on BOTH branches (sick days pay nothing but still cost).
- **Retire precedes sick** — `DaySchedule.build` offers `retire_offer` before the sick short-circuit; a rich sick morning can still retire.
- **Sick day = exactly 1 day** — the schedule collapses to the sick beat; recovery is absolute (`sick_recovery_energy`), not blended.
- **Sick days never touch the rank ladder — by construction.** `RankLadder.evaluate` runs only after `work_pm` (game.gd), and a sick day has no work slots. `StatEffects.close_day` does NOT evaluate the ladder.
- **Rank news lands at the office** — a NarrationBeat right after afternoon work, same day. Never in the day-end summary (user ruling, issue #9).
- **Every ladder/sick consequence telegraphs one day ahead** — warning beat before any demotion, wobble line in the summary the evening before a sick-risk morning.
- **Energy carries over** — `start_next_day` blends leftover energy with the bedtime refill (`energy_carry_weight`); wrecked evenings produce wrecked mornings (user ruling, issue #5).

### Wave-3a invariants (morning clock + triage)

- **The clock is a punishment, never a gate.** It never blocks a slot, never ends the day, and never ends or interrupts a game (drive, drive home and freetime are judged from it once, at slot end). Missing the 8:00 arrival costs a capped `performance` debit at the commute (`lateness_debit_per_min`, `lateness_debit_cap`) — nothing else. The morning is a deadline, not a budget: `work_start_minutes` is the stored value and the budget is DERIVED from it (`cfg.morning_budget()` = `work_start_minutes - morning_start_minutes`). There is no `morning_budget_minutes` — it was retired in QA-R2, and a tunable that reintroduces it splits the authority.
- **`DayClock` is the only time authority, and `game.gd` is its only spender.** `_process` converts real seconds to game-minutes (`clock_scale`); `_on_finished` charges the fixed `skip_time_cost`. No minigame, and no `StatEffects` branch, touches the clock.
- **The morning clock runs for `MORNING_TIMED` only** (`game.gd` const — wake, brush, shower, clothes, breakfast, drive) and the clock label is hidden outside them and `EVENING_TIMED` (the evening clock; the rest of the HUD persists). Once the dog is at the office, time stops mattering.
- **Red before debit is a TELEGRAPH guarantee, not an avoidability one.** `DayClock.zone` turns red before `overrun()` can be non-zero, so lateness is always shown on-screen before it is charged — same telegraph rule as the wave-2 warning beat. It does NOT promise the dog a move that saves the day: the traffic roll is a surprise at the drive (QA-R2 user ruling #3), so a bad roll can show the red and leave nothing to do about it. "Late just because" is the intent, not a regression. Do not "fix" this by forecasting traffic or by softening the roll.
- **Skipping costs time, never saves it.** `SKIP_SLOTS` (`game.gd` const — brush, shower, breakfast) charge `skip_time_cost` on top of the stat penalty. Ducking out is a stat trade, not a clock trade.
- **Mandatory vs skippable is a design contract, not a scene detail.** Skippable: brush, shower, breakfast (SkipButton present). Mandatory, no skip button: clothes, drive. Adding or removing a SkipButton changes the day's shape — `test_scene_smoke.gd` pins the mouse-transparency half for the other scenes (the chores' is `test_morning_props`); the skip half is enforced here.
- **The commute never crashes.** Traffic pins speed and blocks lane switches; it never damages, fails, or ends the run (user ruling). Every crash/collide mention in `lane_sim.gd` is a doc comment asserting the invariant.
- **Traffic is wave-structured, not scattered, and its speed is rolled fresh every commute** — `LaneSim.make_traffic` builds walls down the route and stacks three layers of speed randomness on them: one roll for the whole commute (`drive_day_speed_min`/`_max`), one per wave (`drive_wave_speed_jitter`), one per car (`drive_car_speed_jitter`). Skill is enforced by construction, not by a density knob (QA-R1, issue #7).
  - **A wave may be fully blocked** — `drive_blocked_wave_chance` rolls per wave, and on a hit the wall fills all five lanes. The guaranteed open lane is gone (QA-R2 user ruling #4). Unblocked walls still rotate their open lane, so two consecutive OPEN walls never share one.
  - **Per-car jitter is load-bearing, not flavour** — it is what makes a wall SHEAR APART as it rolls. That shear is the only thing stopping a blocked wall from pinning the dog for the whole remaining route, and `test_a_fully_blocked_wall_shears_apart_instead_of_pinning_the_whole_route` is the test that holds it. Flattening `drive_car_speed_jitter` toward zero is a soft-lock on fun, not a tuning choice.
  - **Cars never pass through each other in-lane**, and it takes TWO mechanisms, not one: `_advance_cars` clamps both speed and position so a long delta cannot tunnel a car through its lead, AND a per-lane separation pass at the end of `make_traffic` pushes the rear car back where generation stacked two waves in one lane. The clamp alone cannot rescue that case — a car already inside the gap freezes rather than separating, so a generation-time overlap would last the whole route.
  - **Spawn positions are back-computed PER WAVE from that wave's own speed**, so each wall still arrives near its intended meet point now that no two waves share a speed. A consequence, on-intent: a faster later wave solves to a smaller spawn, so waves can merge into one thicker obstacle (re-measured at 850 length / top speed 100: 5.1% of walls at 3 walls, 15.2% at 4 — the shipped `drive_waves_base`/`_max`). Under the no-crash invariant a merge is a crawl, never a failure.
- **`_tomorrow_energy` mirrors `GameState.start_next_day` exactly** — including the shower penalty added this wave. It is a read-only projection for the wobble telegraph; any drift between the two formulas is a bug (`game.gd` `_tomorrow_energy` / `game_state.gd` `start_next_day`).
- **Every Control in the click path must be `MOUSE_FILTER_IGNORE`** — `_unhandled_input` never sees an event a Control already ate, and `STOP` is the default. This binds the shell roots (`main.tscn`, `lab.tscn`) as much as the leaf scenes; fixing one leaf is not enough. Buttons are exempt. (issue #6 — shipped broken twice, once per shell.)

### Wave-3b invariants (evening clock + sleep economy)

- **The evening clock runs the evening against a banded bedtime.** `EVENING_TIMED` (`game.gd` const — drive_home, dinner, cooking, freetime) runs one fresh `DayClock` built on ENTERING the block (`_start_slot`), with `set_bands(BANDS, cfg.bed_band_lines())`. Freetime is the evening's till: its end stamps `band` and `bed_minute` onto the result (`_on_finished`'s freetime branch) and `StatEffects` never touches a clock. As with the morning, the span is derived from the stored lines — no stored evening budget.
- **The clock's colour drains toward the next line, and is red past the last line.** `DayClock.zone` measures the band the clock is in (`day_clock.gd:78`), so red arrives before a band is lost; past the final line the segment is infinite and the zone is red (user ruling 2026-10-12: keep).
- **Each game played in free time charges `freetime_game_minutes`.** The minigame only emits `game_played`; `_on_game_played` spends, so `game.gd` stays the clock's only spender. Without this more games never moved the bed minute (issue #12).
- **`SleepLedger` accrues debt, capped both ways.** `record_night` (`sleep_ledger.gd:26`) adds `sleep_need_minutes` minus minutes slept, clamped to `-sleep_bank_cap_minutes`..`sleep_debt_cap_minutes`. The midnight wrap is mandatory: wake minus bed is negative for exactly the late band.
- **The energy ceiling is debt's ONLY consequence path.** `SleepLedger.max_energy` (`sleep_ledger.gd:51`) caches into `GameState.energy_ceiling`, which clamps `apply_energy` and the overnight blend; nothing reads `debt_minutes` to apply a penalty directly (the temptation pull reads it for the habit spiral, a difficulty input, not a penalty). `_tomorrow_energy` mirrors both clamps (`_tomorrow_energy`).
- **A sick day banks `sick_sleep_minutes` — the escape hatch from the sick loop.** A sick day has no freetime slot, so `record_sick_day` (`sleep_ledger.gd:44`, called from `StatEffects.apply`'s sick branch) banks its own long night. Without it a maxed debt pins energy under the sick threshold forever; keep `sick_sleep_minutes` well above `sleep_need_minutes`.
- **The ledger line's rung is the ceiling's clarity tier** (`sleep_ledger.gd:68`): banked / square / running short / running on fumes, cut at `Difficulty.clarity_tier(max_energy)` — the same tier the dog's state text reads, so the copy cannot say "square" while the dog looks tired. It prints once, in the sleep beat; debt is never a HUD gauge.
- **Skipping keeps `skip_time_cost` 8** (`_on_finished`; user ruling 2026-10-12: skip is the wrecked dog's escape). Do not retune it as a sleep lever.
- **Cooking is gated on the dinner choice.** Only `choice == "cook"` hosts the pan (`_on_finished`'s dinner branch), out-of-band like the rank news; takeout costs bone-bucks and no clock, cooking costs clock and no money. The pan, not the dinner walk, pays the cooking energy.

### Wave-A invariants (minigame contract, losses, HUD)

- **A minigame result is `{score, skipped, failed}`; `failed` defaults false.** The `Minigame` base (`minigame.gd`) owns the run flag, finish-once, the SkipButton handler, the countdown and the loss gag.
- **`_fail(gag, extra)` loses the game at once.** Input, skip and countdown go dead, the gag pops centred, and after `fail_gag_seconds` a failed result reports exactly once. `extra` carries a real score for score-based misses (work below `work_fail_score`, lunch no hits, breakfast 0 of 3, cooking's 3rd spoiled step) so the work EMA and the breakfast/cooking energy still read it; a timeout reports score 0.
- **`_start(params)` takes `time_limit`; zero-arg `_start()` is unchanged.** `has_countdown()` feeds %TimerBar through `_timer_bar_fraction()` (`game.gd`), the one place that picks what the bar reads. Drive, drive home and free time have no countdown: their bar reads DayClock time left (to 8:00 / to `home_by_minutes` / to the 23:30 mid line).
- **The loss cost is narrow.** `StatEffects` charges `fail_performance_cost` / `fail_energy_cost` only for `StatEffects.LOSS_COST_SLOTS` (wake, brush, shower, clothes, breakfast, lunch, dinner, cooking). Drive, drive home, work and freetime are excluded because their miss is already charged (lateness debit, the evening clock and sleep, work EMA, sleep ledger), and the coffee cart is no game, so it never fails; `game.gd` stamps drive's, drive home's and freetime's MISS at slot end (`_on_finished`: late arrival / home past `home_by_minutes` / late band) since none has a countdown (user rulings, issue #17).
- **Limits scale with energy.** Each limit lerps `*_time_limit_base` to `*_time_limit_max` on the game's own energy deficit (`Difficulty.*_params`). Breakfast and cooking do not scale, so max == base. Flat limits failed: misses cost energy, and lower energy makes the games harder.
- **The clock never ends or interrupts a game; drive, drive home and freetime are judged from it once, at slot end. The gag and stamp hold both pause it.** `DayClock` does not spend while a hosted game `is_gagging()` or while the stamp holds (`stamp_hold_seconds`); both are presentation. Charging the gag to the clock was tried and rejected (late arrivals 6 to 21).
- **A failed brush or shower forfeits `selfcare_restore` but still counts as done** — no next-morning skip penalty (`StatEffects._restore_unless_lost`).
- **HUD layout (`main.tscn`).** %StampLabel mid-screen shows HIT / MISS / SKIP from `Verdict` (`scripts/ui/verdict.gd`); `Verdict.COUNTED_SLOTS` is the 13 playable slots (incl. drive_home and dinner; the coffee cart is a breather, #18), so beats and cards get none. %StripLabel under the day label marks ✅ / ❌ / – per game (the plain ✓/✗ glyphs are missing in both the default font and Twemoji). %PosterLabel is top-right, "🦴 savings / target to retire"; %RankLabel is top-left. Every HUD node sits after MinigameHost and is mouse-transparent (asserted).

### Wave-B invariants (reworked games, ramp, props)

- **Art slots, not art.** Props come from `Minigame._prop` / `PropArt` (`scripts/ui/prop_art.gd`): `assets/props/<game>/<name>.png` when it imports, else a Twemoji placeholder. A PNG drop-in needs no code. The art is user-generated (#24) — an agent never generates it.
- **The day ramp is speed only.** `Difficulty.day_ramp(progress, ramp_max)` lerps 1.0 to the game's own `day_ramp_{breakfast,burst,triage}_max` (1.3 / 1.74 / 1.1) over `day_progress` (counted slots done / counted today). A time limit never shrinks with progress. Every ramped `*_params` needs a row in `test_difficulty`'s `_ramped_games()`. The lab has a day-progress slider for the three.
- **`home_by_minutes` is the drive home's deadline** (21:46). A miss is charged only through the evening clock and sleep ledger — no generic loss cost. Drive and drive home share top speed (`drive_top_speed` 100); do not split it.
- **`clock_red_fraction` is 0.05** of the morning (the evening bands share it), so a clean sharp morning ends amber at the median, not red.
- **The coffee cart is a breather, not a game** (user ruling 2026-10-14). An untimed `ChoiceCard`: coffee (`coffee_price`, work AM +`coffee_buff_tiers`, then the crash), a snack (`snack_price`, work PM +`snack_buff_tiers`, no crash), both, or water. `BalanceConfig.shop_prices()` derives both's price, never stores it. `_params_for("coffee")` greys out what savings can't cover through `disabled_choices`. No stamp, strip mark or loss.
- **The autoplay bot reads public accessors only** (`session()`, `LaneSim`, a card's buttons), never a game's private fields.
- **Play probes measure the human, not the bot.** Every finished counted game appends one JSON line (`ProbeLog`, `scripts/core/probe_log.gd`), and each breather (`game.gd` `BREATHER_SLOTS`) a choice-only one (slot, choice, start energy, savings), to `user://probes.jsonl` (Windows: `%APPDATA%/Godot/app_userdata/Dog Eat Dog/`) and prints it as `[probe] <json>` (the web build's only readable copy); per-item detail comes from `Minigame.probe_detail()`. Gated by `probes_enabled`, never written under a headless display (GUT, autoplay, CI). Probes observe only: play never reads them, and the bot's reports stay byte-identical. Ride tuning reads these, the bot is a proxy (user ruling 2026-10-14).
